How to stay safe

  • Check the sender's full email address and domain.
  • Do not click unexpected links; visit the official website by typing its address yourself.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or banking information by email.
  • Be cautious with urgent threats, prizes and unexpected attachments.
  • Verify unusual requests through a trusted, separate communication channel.
  • Report suspicious messages to your organization or email provider.

Important: This is a learning prototype using simple text rules, not a trained AI model or a full email security product. It may miss phishing emails or flag legitimate emails. A “No obvious warning signs” result does not prove an email is safe. Sender authenticity, link destinations and attachments require independent verification.
